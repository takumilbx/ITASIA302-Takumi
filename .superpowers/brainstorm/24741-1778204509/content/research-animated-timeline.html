9 years in Thai education
Watching teachers struggle with technology not because they lacked will, but because the system gave them no scaffolding. That gap stayed with me.

Cross-cultural youth leadership across ASEAN. First time I saw how different "technology in school" looked country-to-country, even within Southeast Asia.
Bangkok Metropolitan Administration · 2023
Tracking BMA's education strategy and visualizing the Thai Scholarship Database. Made me realize data exposes what systems prioritize — and what they miss.
