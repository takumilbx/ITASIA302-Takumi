Three ways to restructure the About page

Each handles the new Work content differently — pick what feels right

A

Add a "Work" tab (minimal change)

Keep existing About Me + Thesis tabs. Add a third tab called Work containing TikTok videos, project links, and contact.

[ About Me ]   [ Master's Thesis ]   [ Work ]
✓ Least disruption ✓ Quick to build ✗ Work hidden behind click
B

Restructure into 3 focused tabs ⭐ Recommended

Rename and reorganize into three tabs with clear purpose: Profile (who I am) · Work (what I've made) · Research (thesis, expanded).

[ Profile ]   [ Work ]   [ Research ]
Profile: Bio · Facts · Timeline · Languages
Work: TikTok videos · Projects grid · Contact/links
Research: Expanded thesis · Research question · Methodology
✓ Clearest structure ✓ Work is a first-class section ✓ Research gets room to grow ✗ More reorganization needed
C

Single scrolling page (no tabs)

Remove tabs entirely. One long scroll: Hero → TikTok reel → Projects → Bio/Timeline → Thesis → Contact. Feels more like a personal site than a CV.

↓ Hero + social links
↓ Featured TikTok videos
↓ Projects grid
↓ Bio + timeline (condensed)
↓ Research / Thesis
↓ Contact
✓ Feels like a real portfolio ✓ Everything visible up front ✗ Very long scroll ✗ Biggest rebuild

My recommendation is B — it gives Work a proper home without a full page rewrite, and the Research tab can grow as the thesis develops. Click your pick and let me know in chat.
