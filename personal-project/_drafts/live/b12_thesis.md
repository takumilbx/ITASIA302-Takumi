## [body3] body
This project produced a reading system. The problem it addresses is discontinuity, since I would stop partway through a body of literature after losing track of what I had already read.

## [body4] body
Five sources shaped that outcome. The project began from Forte's definition, a digital archive of one's most valuable memories, ideas and knowledge [[(Forte, 2022, p. 4)]], which this project treats as a position to test. Jones sets the limit of any such tool: knowledge can be managed only indirectly, through the management of information [[(Jones, 2010, Abstract)]]. In Ferreira and colleagues, intended retrieval shapes how knowledge bases get built and used [[(Ferreira et al., 2026, p. 72)]]. Recall outperforms rereading after two days, which is why the reading has to stay mine [[(Roediger & Karpicke, 2006)]]. Luhmann's slip-box predates all of the software [[(Luhmann, 1981, p. 225)]].

## [body5] body
For my own learning the effect is continuity. Each reading now lands against what I have already recorded, so the degree accumulates. The next stage is to extend the structure beyond academic material, to the people I meet, my full time work and my other commitments, so a fuller record can surface more connections. The academic side comes first.
