## [body3] body
I came upon this concept a while ago, long before Claude Code and the whole agentic AI thing. It is a system called Zettelkasten, and it is the earlier version of the second brain system I use now.

## [body4] body
Zettelkasten is German for slip-box, and that is literally what it was, a cabinet of paper cards. The sociologist Niklas Luhmann kept one for 26 years before he wrote about it [[(Luhmann, 1981, p. 223)]], and over its whole life it reached roughly 90,000 cards [[(Schmidt, 2016)]]. He called it a kind of second memory [[(Luhmann, 1981, p. 225)]].
