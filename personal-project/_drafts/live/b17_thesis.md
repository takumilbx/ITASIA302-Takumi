## [body3] body
Three sources carry it. Forte (2022) is the book that made capture-first a movement, studied here rather than leaned on. Luhmann (1981) shows the retrieval-first idea is older than any of the apps. Ferreira and colleagues (2026) supply the field evidence, that "the strategy participants use to create and organize their knowledge is strongly related to their intended retrieval strategy" [[(p. 74)]]. Templates fail when they encode somebody else's retrieval strategy.
