## [body4] body
I started this project wanting a system that would remember for me. What I ended up with does something different. The vault holds what I read and what I thought about it, in pieces small enough to find again, and it hands them back when I go looking. The reading is still mine. The remembering is still mine. What the box adds is a place where the two can meet without me holding everything in my head at once.

## [body5] body
Luhmann's one condition for the box was that it could surprise him [[(Luhmann, 1981, pp. 222, 225)]]. Mine has done it once already, when two notes from different fields met and became my methodology chapter. Once is enough to know the condition holds. I did not build a second brain that thinks for me. I built one that waits. Every paper I read myself puts another note in the box, and every linked note is another chance to be surprised.
