## [body4] body
I started this project wanting a system that would remember for me. What I ended up with does something different. The vault stores what I read and what I thought about it, in small pieces arranged in a way easy enough to find again. The understanding is still mine, and once I read the notes, the remembering of exact details is still mine. What the box adds is a place where those different ideas can meet without me holding everything in my head at once.

## [body5] body
Luhmann's one condition for the box was that it could surprise him [[(Luhmann, 1981, pp. 222, 225)]]. Mine has done it once already, when two notes from different fields converged and became my methodology chapter. In the end, the second brain I built is not the one that thinks for me, but it surprises me every time I see the connection between the notes I put in my slip-box.
