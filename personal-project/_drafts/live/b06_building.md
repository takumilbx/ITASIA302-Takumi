## [blist3] blist
- Not a fancier filing cabinet. Small single-idea notes that connect into *arguments*.
- Most advice obsesses over capture. But how people plan to retrieve is what shapes how their knowledge bases get built and used [[(Ferreira et al., 2026, p. 72)]].
- The memory research agrees: retrieval practice beats re-reading for long-term retention [[(Roediger & Karpicke, 2006)]].
- So: retrieval first, then test the claim honestly.
