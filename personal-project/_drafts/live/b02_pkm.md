## [h22] h2
What is {{PKM}}?

## [body3] body
I cannot remember things. Personal knowledge management is the thing I think could help me.

## [body4] body
For me it is a system that helps me get whatever information I want at the right time. It might not fit every person. Everyone has different learning styles and different ways of handling information, so a personal knowledge management system is one that fits your own style of managing knowledge. The point of this project is finding out what kind of system suits me, and then trying to build it.

## [body5] body
At first I was thinking the other way around. I thought it was a silver bullet that would organise my thoughts in a fancy way, something like Jarvis in the Iron Man movie. It is not about that. The word personal is the important one. The core of personal knowledge management is making it personal, there is no one size fits all solution, and you have to explore your way through it.
