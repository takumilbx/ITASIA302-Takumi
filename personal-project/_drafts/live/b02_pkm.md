What is {{PKM}}?

## [blist3] blist
- Collect, store, *retrieve*, and own the whole thing yourself. That last part is what separates it from a subscription.
- The term goes back only to a 1998 EDUCOM paper by Frand and Hixson [[(Frand & Hixson, 1998)]]. The habit is much older. Leonardo left more than 7,000 notebook pages mixing anatomy, engineering and art [[(Isaacson, 2017)]], and Luhmann's slip-box reached roughly 90,000 cards [[(Schmidt, 2016)]].
- In 1945 Vannevar Bush described the Memex, a desk that stored everything you read and pulled it back along associative trails. The hard part he wrote about was the pulling back [[(Bush, 1945, sect. 6)]].
- Drucker named knowledge work in 1959, and after that managing what you know stopped being optional [[(Drucker, 1959)]].
