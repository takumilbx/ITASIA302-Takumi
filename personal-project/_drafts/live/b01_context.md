## [body3] body
Unlike a normal corporate job, or my bachelor's degree, master's studies rely heavily on me. I have to get everything together and organise it myself. It comes from everywhere. Meetings with my adviser, feedback, reading a single paper, insights from field observation. I cannot track all of it along the way.

## [body4] body
That is what happened in my first semester, and it really frustrated me. I know I had a really good idea, something that might benefit my thesis, and I could not remember it along the way. Then it becomes a loop. I have to read the paper again. I have to have the idea again.

## [body5] body
I am not really good at note taking either, so I keep forgetting things. I wanted to try something that helps me remember better, organise my thoughts, and pull out what I already have more easily.
