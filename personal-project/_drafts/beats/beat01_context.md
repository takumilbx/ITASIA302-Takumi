## Personal essay
I did not start this project because I love note apps. I started it because my thesis reading list stopped fitting in my head. A paper I read in March had to survive until a chapter I would write in November, and "I will remember it" had already failed me twice. Watching T-sensei teach in a classroom full of tablets gave me the same lesson from the other side: expertise is not how much you hold, it is what you can reach for at the right moment. So I stopped collecting and started building for reach.
