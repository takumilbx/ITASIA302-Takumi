## Personal essay
I am not writing this from above the hype. I bought in. I watched the setup videos, downloaded the templates, and rebuilt my vault three times to match other people's screenshots. Each rebuild felt like progress and produced nothing. That is the uncomfortable part of the evidence gap: the industry does not need the systems to work, it needs them to feel like they are about to. My project starts from the question the marketing never answers. Where is the proof?
