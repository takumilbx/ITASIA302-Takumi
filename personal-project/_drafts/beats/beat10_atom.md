## Personal essay
Atomicity is the discipline I resisted longest and need most. My old notes were summaries: five ideas braided together, none of them findable. Splitting feels wasteful in the moment, like using five envelopes for five sentences. Then the payoff arrives months later, when one of those sentences is exactly the missing piece of an argument, and its filename walks it straight into my search results.
