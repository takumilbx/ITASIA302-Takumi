## Personal essay
The pipeline is where I use AI, and where I learned to bound it. NotebookLM answers only from the paper it is given. Claude proposes, but the accept-reject decision runs against the source page, by me. Two of seven candidates get rejected in a typical run, and those rejections are the point: an extraction pipeline without a rejection step is a rumor mill. The seven steps look slow. They are the reason I can cite this essay's own claims down to the page.
